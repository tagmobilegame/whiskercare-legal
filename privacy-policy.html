WhiskerCare Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

WhiskerCare is an iOS app for tracking when your cat has been fed. This policy explains what data the app collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have. It applies to the WhiskerCare app and its supporting services.

Who is responsible for your data

WhiskerCare is operated by the app's developer, trading as Tagu Builds. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact whiskercaresupport@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect what the app needs in order to work. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

DataWhy we collect it
Email address To create and secure your account, and to let household members recognise who is who. Provided when you sign in.
Name or display name So that a shared feeding log can show which person recorded a feed.
Account identifier A unique ID that links your data to your account and your household.
Cat details The cat's name, appearance, feeding schedule and notes that you enter, so the app can show and share feeding status.
Feeding history The time and type of each feed, and who recorded it, so household members can see whether the cat has already eaten.
Household information Household name, membership and invite codes, so that a household can share one feeding log.
Notification token A device token issued by Apple, used only to deliver feeding reminders you have asked for. Collected only if you enable notifications.
Subscription status Purchase identifiers and renewal status received from Apple, used to confirm whether a paid plan is active.

What we do not collect

WhiskerCare does not request access to your camera, photo library, contacts, microphone, or location. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

Payments

Subscriptions are purchased and billed through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see or store your card details. Apple tells our service whether a subscription is active, has renewed, or has expired, so the app can unlock the right features. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings.

Sharing within a household

WhiskerCare is designed for households to share one feeding log. If you join or create a household, other members of that household can see your display name, the cats in the household, and the feeding entries recorded, including who recorded them. Only people who have joined your household can see this information.

Who else processes your data

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

  • Google Firebase — sign-in, database and server functions.
  • Apple — sign-in, subscription billing, and push notification delivery.
  • Stripe — used only if you pay outside the App Store.

Data may be stored on servers located in the United States. Where data is transferred out of the UK or European Economic Area, it is protected by the standard contractual clauses offered by these providers.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data as described below. Server logs used for security and troubleshooting are kept for a short period and then discarded.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app, under Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your profile, your cats, your invite codes, and your household membership. If you are the only member of a household, the household is deleted too.

One thing is deliberately kept: feeding entries you recorded in a household that other people still use remain in that shared log, because removing them would leave gaps in other members' feeding history. Your name is removed from those entries and replaced with "deleted account", so they are no longer linked to you.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, you have the right to access a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to receive it in a portable form. You can exercise most of these directly in the app, or by emailing us at the address above. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority; in the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

WhiskerCare is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
