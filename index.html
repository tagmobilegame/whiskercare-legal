Common questions

How does household sharing work?
One person creates a household and shares the invite code. Everyone who joins sees the same feeding log, so nobody double-feeds the cat. Invited members join free and do not need their own subscription.
Do invited members have to pay?
No. One WhiskerCare Family plan covers the whole household. Only the person who sets the household up subscribes.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel from there. You can also use the manage subscriptions link.
I paid but the app still shows Free.
Open the plan screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. If that doesn't help, email us and include the Apple Account you purchased with.
How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Settings and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and personal data. See the privacy policy for exactly what is removed.
